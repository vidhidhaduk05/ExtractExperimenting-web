# Future marketing plans

## 2026-10-27 — Borrowed-authority search visibility playbook

Status: planning backlog only; no publishing or purchases requested.

Source: user-supplied frame-by-frame inventory of David Roberts's video (@DAVID_D_ROBERTS), promoting The Parasite SEO Playbook. Five tactics were supplied; the other six are unknown. Video claims and cited statistics have not been independently verified.

## Proposed checklist / SOP

1. **Ahrefs — upcoming-year queries.** Research relevant search demand, including 2027 topics. Build useful, evidence-backed content rather than changing a year label. Record audience, query, evidence, and destination page.
2. **Zenodo — genuine research outputs.** Consider a methods paper, dataset, or reproducible benchmark with authorship, methods, limitations, and supporting files. Check repository policies first. Do not disguise advertising as research or fabricate citations.
3. **LinkedIn — concise answer-first articles.** Answer a relevant professional question in the first line. Test the video's under-330-word suggestion as a hypothesis, not a ranking rule. Include sources, affiliation disclosures, and a relevant link.
4. **Gamma — distinct educational decks.** The video proposes 20 decks targeting different queries. Pilot a few useful, original decks before scaling. Verify public access, indexability, and link behavior; avoid repetitive doorway content.
5. **Klint + Reddit — helpful discussion replies.** Evaluate Klint for finding relevant recent threads. Read each discussion and community rules, answer substantively, disclose affiliation where relevant, and mention the product only when appropriate. No bulk spam, fabricated endorsements, or vote manipulation.

## Evidence and rollout gates

- Verify current platform terms, tool availability, publishing requirements, and costs before execution.
- Unverified video claims include page-one rankings in two weeks, automatic authority transfer, guaranteed indexing, Google/ChatGPT trust, Zenodo's 911 citations across 538 pages, LinkedIn citation dominance, and Reddit visibility/16.8% mention share. The video attributes data to Ahrefs, Profound, and SISTRIX; underlying reports and dates were not supplied.
- Use product-relevant questions instead of copying the CRM/mattress examples. Follow Master Theme where applicable and public Co-Scientist terminology; never disclose proprietary internal method names.
- Track publication URL/date, target question, sources, disclosure, index status, referral visits, qualified signups, and observed answer-engine citations with dated evidence. Establish targets from a baseline; no ranking promises.
- Prepare a reviewed topic list, owners, channel-specific drafts, tracking setup, and review date before rollout. Expand only after a useful, policy-compliant pilot has measurable results.
