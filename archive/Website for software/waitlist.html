[ 03 ] QUESTIONS

Common questions about
the closed beta.

We onboard in small cohorts to keep founder support meaningful. Typical wait is 2 – 8 weeks depending on review type and capacity. Methodologists running registered protocols are usually invited first.

Beta access is free. Pricing tiers are listed on the landing page but dollar amounts are intentionally hidden until the model stabilizes. PhD students and RAs will have a free tier sized to cover a thesis-scale review.

No. The tool is positioned as AI-assisted, human-verified. Every extraction is traceable to a sentence-level source, dual-extracted, and referee-checked. The human reviewer is always in the loop. We do not market this as a medical device.

A prospective validation study following the TRIPOD-LLM reporting guideline is in preparation. We do not yet have a published validation paper to cite, and we will not claim accuracy benchmarks until that work is peer-reviewed. Targets used in onboarding materials are framed as aspirational against published literature.

Your uploaded studies and extractions stay in your project workspace. Beta participants are asked for consent to log workflow events (timing, edit patterns) for product improvement; consent is opt-in and revocable. We do not train models on your private corpus.
