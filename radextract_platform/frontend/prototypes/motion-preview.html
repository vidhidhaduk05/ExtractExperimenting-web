Four stages.
No shortcuts.

A clear research process, from the first question to an interpretation another researcher can examine.

  1. The question

    Formulate

    Define the population, intervention or imaging approach, comparator, and outcomes.

  2. The literature

    Search

    Develop database-specific search strategies and document how the evidence was retrieved.

  3. The selection

    Screen

    Apply explicit eligibility criteria and retain a transparent record of decisions.

  4. The interpretation

    Synthesize

    Examine study design, assess limitations, and report what the evidence can support.
